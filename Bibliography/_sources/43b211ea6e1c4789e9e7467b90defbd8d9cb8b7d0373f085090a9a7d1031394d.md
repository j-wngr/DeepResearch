## ABSTRACT

Hypertension is a serious condition that affects both industrialized and developing nations’ public health. It is an important medical condition that can raise the chance of developing heart, brain, kidney, and other problems.

### Objective:

• To evaluate the effect of green tea and black tea on blood pressure (BP). • To compare the levels of BP among black tea- and green tea-consuming hypertensive patients. • To find out the association between effects of green tea- and black tea-consuming hypertensive patients with selected demographic variables.

### Methodology:

A quasiexperimental research design with the purposive sampling technique was adopted to conduct a study among 60 hypertensive patients. Thirty patients consume black tea, and 30 consume green tea. Data were gathered by structured questionnaires using demographic variables and biological parameters.

### Result:

The calculated Student independent ‘t’ test value of t = 3.216 in the post-test shows that there was a statistically significant difference between the post-test levels of systolic BP between the two groups, which was statistically significant at *P* < 0.01 level. The calculated student independent‘t’ test value of t = 2.986 in the post-test shows that there was a statistically significant difference between the post-test levels of diastolic BP between the two groups, which was statistically significant at *P* < 0.01 level.

### Conclusion:

Green tea was found to be more effective than black tea in reducing the level of hypertension among the hypertensive clients.

**KEYWORDS:** *Black tea*, *blood pressure*, *effects*, *green tea*, *hypertensive* *patients*

## INTRODUCTION

Hypertension is a significant public health issue and significant medical condition that develops when the amount of blood the heart pumps and the force with which the blood pumps through the arteries are both abnormal.[[1](#R1)] When the heart beats, blood from the left ventricle rushes into the arteries, where it travels to different parts of the body.[[2](#R2)] Usually, the cardiac output is the blood pressure. 46% of persons with hypertension are considered to be ignorant of their condition. 42% of adult cases of hypertension are identified and treated.[[3](#R3)] 21 percent of persons who have hypertension have it under control.[[4](#R4)] According to a meta-analysis, drinking black tea on a regular basis can lower blood pressure.[[5](#R5)] Despite evidence from animal studies and observational studies showing that consuming green tea can lower blood pressure.[[6](#R6)]

## MATERIALS AND METHODS

A quasi-experimental research design with the purposive sampling technique was adopted in this study. The main study was conducted in the Mappedu village. The sample size comprised 60 hypertensive patients with 30 consuming black tea and 30 consuming green tea. Inclusion criteria are the people having hypertension for the past 3 years and a mild hypertension range of 140–160/90–100. Those who have systemic diseases and moderate and severe hypertension were excluded. The study was explained, and informed consent was obtained. The project has been approved by the ethics committee of the institution.

## RESULTS AND DISCUSSION

The demographic variables of the hypertensive patients are as follows: 17 (56.7%) in Group I and 14 (46.6%) in Group II were aged between 41 and 60 years, 18 (60%) in Group I and 19 (63.3%) in Group II were male, 15 (50%) in Group I and 19 (63.3%) in Group II had primary education, 11 (36.7%) in Group I and Group II were coolie, 20 (66.7%) in Group I and 22 (73.3%) in Group II belonged to a nuclear family, 18 (60%) in Group I and 17 (56.6%) in Group II were sedentary workers, 21 (70%) in Group I and 22 (73.3%) in Group II were married, 24 (80%) in Group I and Group II were nonvegetarian, 18 (60%) in Group I and 21 (70%) in Group II had no bad habits, 30 (100%) both in Group I and Group II were residing in rural areas, 21 (70%) in Group I and 19 (63.3%) in Group II were not consuming tea, and 15 (50%) in Group I and 18 (60%) in Group II had no physical activity.

The study found that green tea significantly reduced systolic and diastolic BP in hypertensive patients in Group I, while black tea significantly reduced it in Group II. There was no significant difference in pretest systolic and diastolic BP levels between the two groups, but a significant difference in post-test systolic and diastolic BP levels was observed in both groups. The results suggest that green tea may be a potential treatment for hypertension tabulated in [Table 1](#jpbs.jpbs_595_24-t001) and [2](#jpbs.jpbs_595_24-t002).
